- A Mac's details in Fleet fold into sections. The admin group stays open; permanent
  admins, integrity, agent, Intune and SAP Privileges show a one-line summary, such as "All
  9 passing", until you open them, and the console remembers which you open. Integrity opens
  by itself when a check fails, permanent admins when one is signed in, and Intune when it
  has no record of the Mac. Integrity alerts show the newest three, with the rest of the
  last ten behind Show all.
